Medians in milliseconds. cpu sim: input, physics, animation, update hooks. cpu draw: walking the scene and recording the frame's commands. gpu span: the time the frame occupied the GPU. ms/frame: wall clock per frame with CPU and GPU overlapped and no vsync wait: the number to quote. Browsers round GPU timestamps to 0.1 ms unless started with --enable-webgpu-developer-features.
