Med Engine 3 · demo level

The Ford

A meadow, a stone terrace, a hut with a lantern and a ferryman who has a story to tell. Everything here is drawn by the engine's own WebGPU renderer, with no three.js and no other library underneath.

starting the renderer

Needs a browser with WebGPU and a keyboard or gamepad. Options: ?quality=low|medium|high|ultra (default: high, lowered automatically if the frame rate is poor) · ?scale=0.6 render scale · ?gpu=1 time the GPU passes (browsers round the timestamps to 0.1 ms; start Chrome with --enable-webgpu-developer-features for exact ones).
