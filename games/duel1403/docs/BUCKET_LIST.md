# Bucket list

Ideas we mean to build, someday. Not now: we keep exploring the game first.

## The story as a movie

Take the role play out of the game and make it a film you live in.

- No game controls, no HUD: you **type what you say and what you do** (or pick
  from suggestions Claude writes for that moment), and the story answers.
- **Nothing scripted.** Every line, every reaction, every turn of the story is
  written live by Claude from who the people are, what they feel and what you
  have done, the way the town's people already talk and the storyteller already
  opens matters.
- **Cinematic:** each moment staged like a film shot: framing, light, close-ups on
  faces, the camera cutting to whoever speaks; scenes and transitions instead of
  walking around.
- **Your own story:** you begin your life (or a story you choose), and your choices
  and words shape it: who loves you, who hates you, what becomes of you.
- People with **warmth, creativity and emotion**, like real humans.

What already exists to build it from: the persona and memory system
(`src/life/Dialogue.js`, `Memory.js`), the storyteller and chronicle
(`Story.js`), your past (`lore.js`), deeds in your own words (`Deeds.js`), the
year as it happened (`history.js`), and the Med Engine's cameras, depth of field
and lighting for the shots.
