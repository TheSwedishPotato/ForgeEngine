BATMAN: VESPER

EPISODE 1 · FIRST NIGHT
Every Earth gets a Batman. This one is just beginning.

BATMAN: VESPER is a fan-made interactive film: an original story on a new Earth. Batman is a DC character; this is a non-commercial fan project.

Engine: Med Engine 2 (WebGL2, deferred PBR, tiled lights, skin scattering, rain, film camera). People built from the CC0 MakeHuman/MPFB assets (a few community garments CC-BY, see CREDITS.md); textures and props from Poly Haven (CC0). Sound and score synthesised live. Story by Claude; free text and Night Two written live by Claude.

Keys: 1–4 choose · type to say anything · letters when they appear on screen (QTE) · WASD explore · E examine · Esc pause · F3 engine stats
