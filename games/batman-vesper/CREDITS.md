## Places

Textures (concrete, brushed concrete, concrete wall, anti-slip concrete, castle wall slates, grey
roof tiles, metal plate, container side) and models (covered car, barrel, wooden crate, street lamp,
caged hanging light, gothic statue, lion head, ocean buoy) from **Poly Haven**, CC0
(https://polyhaven.com). Everything else (the cathedral, the garage, the van and cars, the Acheron,
the Nightjar, the sea, the city) is modelled in code.

## Sound

All synthesised in the browser (WebAudio): no recordings.
